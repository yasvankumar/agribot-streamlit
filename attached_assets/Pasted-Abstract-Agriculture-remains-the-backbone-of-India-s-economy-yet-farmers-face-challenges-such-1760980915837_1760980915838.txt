Abstract
 • Agriculture remains the backbone of India’s economy, yet farmers face challenges 
such as lack of expert guidance, limited regional language support, delayed 
disease diagnosis, and fragmented advisory systems. Existing solutions often 
handle one problem at a time—either disease detection or market updates—but 
lack integration and multilingual accessibility.
 • AgriBot is designed as a bilingual (Tamil & English) AI-powered virtual 
assistant that integrates Machine Learning (for crop disease detection), 
anomaly detection models (for unseen/rare issues), Natural Language 
Processing (for conversational support), and real-time APIs (weather + 
market prices) into a single accessible platform via WhatsApp and Web.
Introduction
 • Agriculture contributes significantly to India’s GDP, yet farmers in rural regions 
still rely on traditional methods.
 • Common challenges:
 • Lack of immediate expert advisory in regional languages.
 • Crop losses due to late disease and pest identification.
 • Fragmented systems → separate apps for disease detection, weather, and 
market updates.
 • Research shows that multilingual support improves adoption of digital tools 
among farmers.
 • AgriBot addresses these challenges by providing an integrated, farmer-friendly 
solution.
Product Vision Statement
 • To build a scalable, bilingual, AI-driven virtual assistant that combines disease 
detection, anomaly detection, real-time advisory, and conversational support into a 
single accessible platform, helping farmers improve yield, reduce crop loss, and 
adopt sustainable farming practices.

 Product Backlogs
 • Train CNN/YOLO on PlantVillage dataset (~54k images, 14 crops, 26 diseases).
 • Classify crop disease from uploaded leaf images.
 • Anomaly Detection
 • Autoencoder + Isolation Forest for unknown patterns.
 • Helps flag new diseases, nutrient deficiencies, or pest attacks.
 • Conversational Agent
 • Tamil-English support using LLM fine-tuning or RAG.
 • Understands farmer queries → responds with contextual advice.
 • Advisory Hub Integration
 • Weather API (OpenWeather/IMD) → forecasts & rainfall prediction.
 • Market API (Agmarknet, mandi prices) → real-time price updates.
 • Cultivation techniques & fertilizer schedules.
 • Accessibility
 • WhatsApp bot (low data usage, rural-friendly).
 • Web interface for broader access
